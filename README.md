Product Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard (Advanced) - Task 2---> Project Overview

This project involves an advanced analysis of a retail dataset to evaluate multi-dimensional performance across regions, product categories, and customer segments. By integrating complex data sources, the project identifies critical revenue drivers and highlights the impact of discounting on overall business profitability to support data-driven decision-making.  

---> Tools UsedExcel → Advanced data cleaning, calculated columns, and pivot table analysis.  SQL (PostgreSQL) → Data transformation and complex joins for analytical practice.  Power BI → Development of an interactive dashboard featuring slicers, KPIs, and trend visualizations.  

---> Key InsightsRegional Dominance: The West region is the primary revenue driver, generating $5.31 Million, followed by the East and Central regions.  Profit Leaders: Technology is the most profitable category with a 17.75% profit margin, whereas Furniture shows a significantly lower margin of 2.75%.  The Discount Paradox: There is a sharp decline in profitability tied to discounts; non-discounted orders average $66.81 in profit, while discounted orders result in an average loss of -$6.19.  Customer Contribution: The Consumer segment is the most valuable, contributing over 51% ($8.41M) of the total sales volume.  Seasonal Trends: Sales exhibit a major "Year-End" surge, peaking in November ($2.53M) and December ($2.41M).  

---> Business RecommendationsOptimize Profitability: Re-evaluate the pricing and logistical costs for the Furniture category to address its low-profit margins.  Refine Discounting: Implement a controlled discount strategy to prevent the current trend of net losses on discounted transactions.  Regional Strategy: Increase marketing investments in the West and East regions while investigating the causes of underperformance in the South.  Segment Retention: Develop loyalty programs specifically for the Consumer segment to maintain its high contribution to total revenue.  Inventory Planning: Leverage identified seasonal peaks to optimize inventory and staffing, particularly for the September-to-December window.
